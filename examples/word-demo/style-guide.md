# House style

- Product name is exactly **Acme Relay** (not "acme relay", "the Relay",
  "Acme relay").
- Spell out numbers under 10; use digits for 10 and above.
- Use the Oxford comma.
- No exclamation marks in internal announcements.
- Prefer active voice and short sentences.
- Dates are written like "3 June 2026", not "6/3/26".
